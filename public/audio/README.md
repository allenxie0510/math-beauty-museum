# Garden music

`mozart-garden.mp3` is a transcoded copy of the 16-second recording
“MozartPianoConcertoNo25ShortShortShortLongMotif.ogg” by Opus33, sourced from
Wikimedia Commons. The composition and recording are released into the public
domain by the recording's creator.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MozartPianoConcertoNo25ShortShortShortLongMotif.ogg

`fur-elise.mp3` is a 45-second excerpt of “FurElise.ogg”, performed by V Gao.
The performer released the recording under CC0/public domain.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:FurElise.ogg

`minuet-in-g.mp3` is a 45-second excerpt of Beethoven's Minuet in G, performed
by Peter Johnston. The recording is identified by Wikimedia Commons as public
domain/free use with permission confirmed by VRT.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Minuet_in_G_(Beethoven),_piano.ogg

# Sound hall ambience

`birdsong-morning.mp3` is a 28-second excerpt of “Bird singing.ogg” by jc. The
author released the natural recording into the public domain.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bird_singing.ogg

`flowing-stream.mp3` is derived from “Hemlock stream.ogg” by Dirtslayer. The
author released the natural recording into the public domain.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hemlock_stream.ogg
